Math Rescue Privacy

Last updated: August 30, 2026

Math Rescue is a free educational puzzle game. We collect only what is needed to save your progress and show leaderboards.

What we store

  • Nickname — the display name you choose (3–8 characters).
  • Game progress — levels unlocked, scores, stars, tutorial status, and daily challenge stats.
  • Device cache — the same data is saved in your browser so you can play offline.
  • Auth token — a random token stored on your device so only you can update your cloud save.

What we do not collect

  • No email address, phone number, or password.
  • No precise location or contacts.
  • No third-party advertising trackers.

How data is used

Your nickname and scores appear on optional leaderboards. Progress is synced to our database (hosted on Neon Postgres) when you are online. We do not sell your data.

Children

The game is designed for learners. We do not knowingly collect personal information beyond a self-chosen nickname. Parents or teachers may contact us to request deletion of a nickname and associated progress.

Deletion

Use Settings → Reset progress in the game to clear local data and delete your cloud profile. You can also clear site data in your browser.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue on the project repository or contact the site owner through the same channel used to publish the game.
